## Featured engineering projects

- **[Custom STM32 drone flight controller](https://github.com/Zceeamt/custom-stm32-drone-flight-controller)**  
  Designed a four-layer STM32H743 flight-controller PCB with an ICM-42688-P IMU, DPS368 barometer, USB-C, SWD and motor interfaces. Integrated it with a custom PLA/TPU airframe, configured Betaflight and completed outdoor flight testing.

- **[Four-layer BLDC electronic speed controller](https://github.com/Zceeamt/four-layer-bldc-esc)**  
  Developed a sensorless three-phase ESC around an STM32F051 and DRV8300 gate driver, including the MOSFET power stage, back-EMF sensing, current measurement and board-level power design.

- **[FPGA UART transmitter](https://github.com/Zceeamt/fpga-uart-transmitter)**  
  Implemented an 8N1 UART transmitter in SystemVerilog for a Cyclone IV FPGA and verified its output in hardware with an Arduino receiver.

- **[Dual-output DC-DC power supply](https://github.com/Zceeamt/dual-output-dc-dc-power-supply)**  
  Designed and simulated 5 V and 12 V MC34063A converters with transistor current limiting, transient verification and component-level analysis in Multisim.
